Our Story

Crafting Visual Stories
Since 2024

SOFILTERED was born from a passion for unfiltered creativity and bold storytelling. We believe that every brand has a unique story waiting to be told through powerful visual design.

Our studio brings together talented designers, illustrators, and creative strategists who share a common vision: to create work that not only looks beautiful but also drives meaningful connections between brands and their audiences.

We believe in unfiltered creativity—raw, authentic, and bold. Our work speaks louder than words, and our designs tell stories that resonate.
