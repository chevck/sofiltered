Our Story

Crafting Visual Stories
Since 2024

SOFILTERED was born from a passion for unfiltered creativity and bold storytelling. We believe that every brand has a unique story waiting to be told through powerful visual design.

Our studio brings together talented designers, illustrators, and creative strategists who share a common vision: to create work that not only looks beautiful but also drives meaningful connections between brands and their audiences.

We believe in unfiltered creativity—raw, authentic, and bold. Our work speaks louder than words, and our designs tell stories that resonate.

How We Think

Built to Stand Out

Creative, but with intention

We're not here to make things look good just for the sake of it. We start with the WHY, then figure out the best way to bring it to life.

We like the expected

There's usually more than one way to solve a creative problem. We explore different angles, push ideas a little further, and look for what makes your brand feel like you.

The little things matter to us

A typeface, a colour, a transition, a caption; it all adds up. We pay attention to the details because they're often what make the bigger picture work.

Beautiful and functional

Beautiful work matters, but so does what it achieves. We create meaningful brand experiences that connect with people and support real growth.
